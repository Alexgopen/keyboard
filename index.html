Draw a melody

Each grid cell is one eighth note. Click once for an eighth note; drag horizontally to sustain across additional eighth-note cells. Measures are 8 cells (4/4). Writing into the last measure automatically adds another measure. Emoji notation is step-based: every (...) is exactly one eighth-note step. Notes that begin on a step go inside the parentheses; each note may have its own sustain such as (❤️x4🔥x2). Empty () is a true silent rest. (~) means no new note begins on this step because one or more earlier notes are still sustaining. Export adds readability-only measure markers like |1|, |2|, |3|; import ignores all |#| markers and writes notes strictly in notation order.
